Combat-ready off-road tactical vehicle. Used by the [[MC]] to travel. Can be used as hard-cover, but it not armed. Best for defensive regrouping, can be used as mobile cover.

# Design Points

- Enclosed space for the player
- Flat front for ramming things
- Off-road capable, four wheels with high suspension
- Military style, heavily armored
